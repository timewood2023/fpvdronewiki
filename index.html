02 / Getting started

Three steps to a session worth flying

No account, no signup — everything is a plain page you can read before you plug in a radio.

Pick a simulator

Start from the selection guide, not from a store page. Match the sim to the flying you want to practise.

Get the controller talking

Follow the connection & calibration guide once, properly. Wrong channels waste entire evenings.

Fly one repeatable goal

Use the first-session checklist: one drill, one line, five minutes of recovery practice.
