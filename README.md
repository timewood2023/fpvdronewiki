# FPV Simulator Wiki

**Less crashing. More flying.**

An independent, source-checked wiki about FPV drone simulators — what each one actually ships, how to set up a radio, and how to turn a five-minute session into repeatable practice.

**[Visit fpvdrone.wiki →](https://fpvdrone.wiki/)**

---

## Why this exists

Most FPV simulator content is either a launch-day review or a feature list copied from a store page. That is not much help on a Tuesday night when your sticks feel wrong and you don't know whether it's the rates, the calibration, or the sim.

fpvdrone.wiki documents simulators the way you'd want them documented *before* a session: documented modes, environment and track tools, controller behaviour, and one concrete drill you can repeat. Pages are dated, and revised when a vendor ships something that changes the answer.

- **Source-checked** — claims trace back to official docs, patch notes or hands-on time, not to a press kit.
- **Practice-first** — every guide ends with something to fly, not something to configure.
- **Independent** — no manufacturer sponsorship of editorial content. Affiliate links are disclosed on the site.

## Start here

1. **Pick a simulator** — use the [selection guide](https://fpvdrone.wiki/compare/best-fpv-simulator/) instead of a store page, so the sim matches the flying you want to practise.
2. **Get the controller talking** — follow the [connection and calibration guide](https://fpvdrone.wiki/guides/controller-setup/) once, properly. Wrong channel mapping wastes entire evenings.
3. **Fly one repeatable goal** — the [first-session checklist](https://fpvdrone.wiki/guides/beginner-guide/) gives you one drill, one line, five minutes of recovery practice.

## Contributing

Corrections are welcome, especially simulator version changes: if a patch adds, removes or renames a mode, environment or tool, open an issue with the version number and a source link. Content fixes land on the wiki first, then get reflected here.
